server/src/suggestionsService.js — smart suggestions

~16 MIN_MONTHSMonths of harvest needed before a crop gets suggestions (4).
~24 suggestions()Builds, per crop and month: yield per m² per 30 days (harvest ÷ crop-cycle area), average temperature & humidity (climate log), realised price. Then: now (this month's temperature vs best range), upcoming (same month last year vs normal), season (best months, best temperature range = top third of months, transplant month = best month − lead time from crop cycles), avoid (worst month), price (best price month). Same-month messages are merged; returns the season calendar (profiles[].byMonth, tempRow).

6. Business rules in one place

  • Sale amount = (quantity − rejected) × rate. Rate = vendor price list (latest effective date) → else crop default price. Only owner / manager can type a different rate.
  • No negative quantities / amounts (except stock adjustments, opening balances, temperature); rejected ≤ delivered; paid ≤ bill; deductions ≤ pay; dates must be real dates.
  • A record used by other records (crop, vendor, farm, worker…) cannot be deleted — mark it inactive.
  • Smart suggestions need 4+ months of harvest per crop; yield is compared per m² per 30 days using crop-cycle area; temperature comes from the climate log.
  • Vendor can confirm or report rejected quantity within 7 days; amount is recalculated (accepted / disputed).
  • Overdue = vendor balance minus sales still inside credit days. Ageing allocates the balance to the oldest sales first.
  • Spray with PHI days locks harvest of that bay (or whole farm) until date + PHI; owner can override.
  • Purchase with a stock item → stock in; issue in Stock in / out. Paid by "credit" or part-paid → payable in balance sheet.
  • Recurring payments with auto-post are added to expenses when due (checked on dashboard load).
  • Wages: daily = days × wage; monthly = salary/30 × days − absent; per kg = kg × rate; OT = hours × day wage / 8. Paying posts gross + bonus to Labour expense and recovers advances.
  • Cost per crop = direct (tagged to crop cycle) + shared costs incl. depreciation split by area × days of running cycles.
  • Balance-sheet cash = opening cash + money in − money out since books-start date.
  • Module off → screens hidden and API blocked; data kept.
